Civility is one of a cluster of societal virtues, along with traits such as:

- Modesty
- Humility
- Politeness

The word **civility** comes from the Latin *civis*, meaning **citizen**, as opposed to *barbarus*, meaning **foreigner**.

Roman citizens were supposed to settle their differences by talking things through and going to court.

Those foreign to Rome were thought to settle their differences by shouting and fighting.

## Civility as a Precondition of Civilization

Abrams argues that civility is a precondition of civilization.

Civility is what allows us to cooperate by overcoming the ordinary **frictions** of everyday life with other people.

On this view, civility is not just politeness. It is one of the things that makes shared life possible.

## Civility as a Fantasy

Gay argues that civility is a fantasy that requires us to “forget who we are.”

On this view, civility can become a way for bigotry to continue existing, as long as it is performed in the correct way.

In other words, civility may not remove injustice. It may simply make injustice sound polite.

## Erasmus and Luther

Compare this debate to the debate between Erasmus and Luther.

Erasmus thought that religious differences could be settled politely, without name-calling.

Luther argued that there is no need to hold back when you know the truth.
