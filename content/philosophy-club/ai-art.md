## Is It Art?

The core dilemma: does the production of art require a certain internal state in the producer, such as consciousness, suffering, or intention? Or is the aesthetic experience of the viewer enough?

## Internal State Theories

These theories argue that art depends on something going on inside the artist.

### Agential Art

Art requires agency guided by “knowledge-how,” not the mechanical aggregation of rules.

### Art as Genius

Art requires genius: a distinctly human capacity to produce original works.

### Embodied Experience

Art requires the embodied experience of the artist.

## Aesthetic Experience Theories

These theories argue that art can be understood through the experience, interpretation, or recognition of the audience and culture.

### Readymade Analogy

Art becomes art through cultural framing.

### Novelty Theories

AI can create work that is novel and surprising, and novelty is central to creativity.

### Recognition Theories

AI-created works have received recognition from the art world.

### P-Creativity

AI can create works that are psychologically creative: it can produce works that are novel to the system itself.

## Is It Ethical?

The core dilemma: to make AI models more powerful, developers train them on billions of images scraped from the internet. Is this the future of innovation, or is it mass theft?

## Theft

These arguments emphasize exploitation, consent, and harm to artists.

### Unauthorized Scraping

AI is trained on data sets containing billions of images, many of which are used without the original creators’ consent.

### Style Theft

AI can replicate an artist’s style with extreme accuracy at little cost.

### Job Threat

Technology companies benefit while artists lose work, and we risk weakening our cultural heritage.

## Innovation

These arguments emphasize transformation, novelty, and expanded access to creative tools.

### Transformative Fair Use

AI does not store libraries of images in the ordinary sense. It trains on massive amounts of data and uncovers statistical patterns.

### Novelty

AI produces novel works by combining different elements, not simply by collaging existing images together.

### Democratization

AI gives creative agency to the digital proletariat, allowing more people to create images and participate in visual culture.
