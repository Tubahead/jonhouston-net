---
title: Meaning in Life
---

## Hinduism

The meaning of life is to realize one’s true self as identical with ultimate reality, and to fulfill one’s duty in the journey toward liberation from the cycle of rebirth.

## Buddhism

The meaning of life is to overcome suffering by following the Noble Eightfold Path, thereby attaining enlightenment and liberation from the cycle of rebirth.

## Judaism

The meaning of life is to walk with God by living righteously, observing the commandments, and participating in the ongoing repair of the world.

## Christianity

The meaning of life is to love and serve God and others, to follow the teachings of Jesus, and to seek eternal life with God through faith, grace, and redemption.

## Plato

The meaning of life is to seek truth and wisdom by turning the soul away from the illusions of the material world and toward the eternal forms, ultimately aligning oneself with the Form of the Good.

## Aristotle

The meaning of life is to achieve flourishing by cultivating virtue through rational activity in accordance with our nature as reasoning beings.

## Epicurus

The meaning of life is to attain lasting happiness and peace by seeking pleasure wisely, avoiding unnecessary pain, and cultivating a tranquil mind free from fear and anxiety.

## Confucius

The meaning of life is to cultivate virtue, fulfill one’s roles and responsibilities within relationships, and live in harmony with others to create a just and orderly society.

## Questions

1. Some philosophers argue that life has no inherent meaning, and that we must create our own meaning in our lives. Is this a scary prospect or a freeing prospect?

2. Are some things more likely to produce meaning in life, or can anything produce meaning?

3. How should we go about finding meaning in life?

4. What role does suffering play in life?

5. What role does mortality play in life?

6. If everyone has a different idea about the meaning of life, how do we coordinate and cooperate? What happens when one person’s meaning of life runs contrary to another person’s meaning of life?
