## Definitions

### Determinism

**Determinism** is the view that every event is determined by some other event, including our deliberations and actions.

### Libertarianism

**Libertarianism** is the view that if we have free will, then we must be able to act differently than we did.

### Compatibilism

**Compatibilism** is the view that if the following statement is true, then we have free will:

> If we had deliberated differently, we would have acted differently.

On this view, we can have free will regardless of the fact that we could not have deliberated differently.
